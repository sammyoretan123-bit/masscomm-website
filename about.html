About the Department

Learn more about Mass Communication

Who We Are

The Department of Mass Communication is one of the leading departments in the institution, dedicated to training students in journalism, broadcasting, public relations, advertising, and media studies. Our curriculum blends theory with practical training to produce well-rounded media professionals.

Our Mission

To provide quality education in mass communication that equips students with the knowledge, skills, and ethical values needed to excel in the media industry and contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in media education, research, and practice — producing graduates who are globally competitive and socially responsible.

Programmes Offered

  • B.Sc. Mass Communication
  • Ph.D. in Mass Communication
  • Diploma in Mass Communication
