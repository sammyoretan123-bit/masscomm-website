The Department of Mass Communication, Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko (AAUA), is pleased to announce the upcoming Freshers Orientation Week for all newly admitted students of the 2026/2027 academic session.

The orientation programme is designed to formally welcome our newest students into the Mass Communication family and to introduce them to the academic culture, facilities, and opportunities available within the department. It will also serve as an opportunity for freshers to meet their lecturers, senior colleagues, and the executive members of the student association.

Activities lined up for the week include a welcome address by the Head of Department, an introduction to the departmental curriculum, a campus tour, an interactive session with lecturers, and a social gathering to help new students settle in comfortably.

All freshers are strongly encouraged to attend every session, as important information regarding course registration, departmental guidelines, and student welfare will be shared.

Date: To be announced

Venue: Mass Communication Lecture Hall

Time: 10:00 AM prompt

For further enquiries, please contact the Public Relations Officer of the Mass Communication Student Association.

We warmly welcome all our freshers and wish them a successful academic journey ahead.
