subtitle: Learn more about Mass Communication
who_we_are: The Department of Mass Communication is one of the leading departments in the institution, dedicated to training students in journalism, broadcasting, public relations, advertising, and media studies. Our curriculum blends theory with practical training to produce well-rounded media professionals.
mission: To provide quality education in mass communication that equips students with the knowledge, skills, and ethical values needed to excel in the media industry and contribute positively to society.
vision: To be a centre of excellence in media education, research, and practice — producing graduates who are globally competitive and socially responsible.
programmes:
  - B.Sc. Mass Communication
  - Phd. in Mass Communication
  - Postgraduate programmes
student_association: The Mass Communication Student Association is the umbrella body for all students in the department. Led by an elected President and EXCO members, the association organises social activities such as sports, press week, freshers orientation, and other events that enrich student life.
