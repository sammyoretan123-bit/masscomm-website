When the referee's whistle blows and the ball finds the back of the net, one name keeps echoing across the field — Samuel Oretan, the striker who has rewritten the record books for the Department of Mass Communication.

This season, Samuel achieved what no Namacosite has ever done before: finishing as the highest goal scorer not just within the Mass Communication Department, but across the entire Faculty of Social Sciences. It is a feat that speaks not only to his individual brilliance but also to the spirit of excellence that defines our department.

Representing the Mass Communication Department in inter-level and faculty competitions, Samuel has been a constant threat to every defence he has faced. His pace, positioning, and clinical finishing have earned him the respect of teammates, opponents, and spectators alike. Match after match, he has delivered when it mattered most — turning tight games into victories and giving our department a proud place on the football map of AAUA.

But beyond the goals, it is his humility, discipline, and team spirit that stand out. Samuel embodies what it means to be a true Namacosite: hardworking, focused, and always ready to represent the department with pride.

His journey is a reminder to every student that greatness is built in the small moments — the early morning training sessions, the sacrifices made, and the determination to keep pushing even when the odds are stacked against you.

The entire Mass Communication family celebrates Samuel Oretan today. His record is not just a personal achievement — it is a victory for every student who wears the colours of our great department.

Congratulations, Samuel. The pitch is yours, and the records are still falling.

— Mass Communication Student Association
