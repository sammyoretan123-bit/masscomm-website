description: |-
  The Broadcasting sequence trains students in the art of radio and television production, on-air presentation, and digital broadcast media. It equips future broadcasters with the technical skills, editorial judgment, and creative vision required to excel in today's fast-paced media landscape.

  Students learn to write for broadcast, produce shows, present on air, edit audio and video, and manage the entire production process from concept to broadcast.

  From news anchoring to documentary production, the Broadcast sequence gives students hands-on experience with cameras, microphones, editing software, and studio equipment — preparing them for careers in radio, television, online streaming, and multimedia journalism.
