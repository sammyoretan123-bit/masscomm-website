description: |-
  The Public Relations and Advertising (PRAD) sequence trains students in the art of strategic communication, brand management, corporate reputation, and persuasive messaging. It prepares students for careers in public relations agencies, corporate communication departments, advertising firms, and media consultancies.

  Through practical coursework, real-world projects, and hands-on campaigns, students learn to build and protect brand images, manage crises, and craft impactful messages that resonate with target audiences.

  The PRAD sequence blends creativity with strategy. Students develop skills in campaign design, media buying, copywriting, event management, and stakeholder relations — preparing them for the fast-paced world of modern communication.
