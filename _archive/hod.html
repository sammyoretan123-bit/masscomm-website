Welcome Message from the HOD

Welcome to the Department of Mass Communication. Our department is committed to training the next generation of journalists, broadcasters, PR practitioners, and media scholars. We combine academic excellence with hands-on experience to prepare our students for the fast-changing world of media and communication.


Whether you are a prospective student, a current student, or a visitor, we are glad to have you here. Feel free to explore our website and learn more about our programmes, staff, and vibrant student community.


— DR. ISHOLA LAMIDI, Head of Department
