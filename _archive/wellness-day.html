Mental Health & Wellness Day

Taking care of our minds and bodies.

Mental health matters. Every year we dedicate a day to wellness — with talks from professionals, relaxation activities, and open conversations about mental health. It’s our way of reminding each other that we are more than just students; we are human beings who need care too.
