Courses / Programmes

What we offer in the Department of Mass Communication

Programmes Offered

  • B.Sc. Mass Communication (4 years)
  • Diploma in Mass Communication
  • Postgraduate Diploma (PGD)
  • M.Sc. Mass Communication
MAC 101

Introduction to Mass Communication

An overview of the field of mass communication, its history, concepts, and role in society.

MAC 102

News Writing and Reporting

Principles and practice of gathering, writing, and editing news for print and digital media.

MAC 201

Broadcast Journalism

Radio and television news production, scripting, and presentation techniques.

MAC 202

Public Relations

Theories and practices of public relations, media relations, and corporate communication.

MAC 301

Media Law and Ethics

Legal and ethical issues affecting journalists and media practitioners.

MAC 302

Advertising and Copywriting

Principles of advertising, campaign design, and creative copywriting for various media.

MAC 401

Research Methods in Communication

Quantitative and qualitative research methods used in communication studies.

MAC 402

Final Year Project

Independent research project supervised by a lecturer, submitted in partial fulfilment of the degree.
