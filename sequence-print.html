Print Journalism

Print Sequence

About the Sequence

The Print Journalism sequence trains students in the timeless craft of newspaper and magazine journalism. It builds strong foundations in news reporting, feature writing, editing, investigative journalism, and editorial design — the core skills that power every newsroom in the world.


Students learn to gather information, verify facts, write compelling stories, edit content for clarity and accuracy, and produce print and digital publications that inform and engage readers.

What You’ll Learn

  • News Writing and Reporting
  • Feature Writing and Long-Form Journalism
  • Investigative Reporting
  • Editorial Writing and Column Writing
  • Editing and Proofreading
  • Layout and Newspaper Design
  • Online Journalism and Digital Publishing
