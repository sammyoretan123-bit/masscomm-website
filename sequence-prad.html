Public Relations & Advertising

PRAD Sequence

About the Sequence

The Public Relations and Advertising (PRAD) sequence trains students in the art of strategic communication, brand management, corporate reputation, and persuasive messaging. It prepares students for careers in public relations agencies, corporate communication departments, advertising firms, and media consultancies.

Through practical coursework, real-world projects, and hands-on campaigns, students learn to build and protect brand images, manage crises, and craft impactful messages that resonate with target audiences. The PRAD sequence blends creativity with strategy.

What You'll Learn

  • Principles and practice of Public Relations
  • Advertising and Copywriting
  • Corporate Communication and Brand Management
  • Campaign Design and Media Buying
  • Event Management and Stakeholder Relations
